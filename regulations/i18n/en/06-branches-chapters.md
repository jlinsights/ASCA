# Regulations on the Establishment and Operation of Branches and Chapters

> [Effective 2026. 2. 13.] [No. 1, 2026. 2. 13., new enactment]

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 (Purpose)

Pursuant to Article 2, Paragraph ② and Article 41 of the "Articles of
Incorporation of the Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts," these Regulations set
out the detailed matters necessary for the establishment, operation, activities,
finances, and management of the Society's branches and chapters (hereinafter
"branches, etc."), with the aim of securing the efficiency and fairness of
organizational operations and the stability of nationwide activities.

### Article 2 (Definitions)

1. **Branch (지부):** Established centered on wide-area administrative units
   (special cities, metropolitan cities, and provinces), and refers to a
   wide-area organization that oversees the Society's activities in the relevant
   region.
2. **Chapter (지회):** Established centered on basic administrative units
   (cities, counties, and districts), and refers to a basic organization that
   carries out practical activities in a specific region under the management
   and supervision of a branch.
3. **Full Member:** Refers to a Full Member under Article 2, Paragraph ③ of the
   Regulations on Membership and Dues.
4. The Gwangju Branch, Cheongju Branch, and Chungju Branch specified in Article
   2, Paragraph ② of the Articles of Incorporation shall be regarded as branches
   under these Regulations, regardless of the administrative unit in which they
   are located.

### Article 3 (Scope of Application)

The operation and activities of branches, etc. shall follow the Articles of
Incorporation and these Regulations. Matters not specified in these Regulations
shall follow the resolutions of the Board of Directors and applicable laws.

## Chapter 2 Establishment and Accreditation

### Article 4 (Establishment Criteria)

1. A region in which a branch is to be established must have at least **20 Full
   Members** who reside there and are active there.
2. A region in which a chapter is to be established must have at least **10 Full
   Members** who reside there and are active there, and must receive a
   recommendation from the branch with jurisdiction over that region.

### Article 5 (Application for Accreditation and Required Documents)

A person intending to establish a branch, etc. shall prepare the following
documents and apply to the Society for accreditation.

1. Application for accreditation of the establishment of a branch (chapter)
   (including the roster of the representative and promoters)
2. Roster of Full Members in the region (including supporting documents of
   activity)
3. Initial annual business plan and budget proposal
4. Supporting documents regarding the election of the branch head

### Article 6 (Accreditation Review and Procedures)

1. The Society's Secretariat shall review the adequacy of the application
   documents within 30 days of receiving them, and may conduct an **on-site
   inspection** where necessary.
2. The result of the review shall be submitted to the Society's Board of
   Directors and must receive final approval.
3. A branch, etc. approved by the Board of Directors shall be issued an
   **accreditation certificate (인준서)** in the Society's name and registered
   as an official organization of the Society. The date of accreditation is the
   date of the Board's resolution.

## Chapter 3 Rights and Obligations

### Article 7 (Rights of Branches, etc.)

Branches, etc. have the following rights.

1. **Right to use the Society's name:** The right to publicize activities in the
   relevant region externally, using the Society's name (Asian Society of
   Calligraphic Arts). However, the purposes and scope of use shall be in
   accordance with Article 11.
2. **Right to apply for activity support funds:** The right to apply, in
   accordance with the regulations, for the **"Branch Activity Support Fund"**
   provided in the Society's budget and for project subsidies.
3. **Right to submit opinions:** The right to submit in writing the opinions of
   the branch, etc., on agenda items submitted to the Society's General Assembly
   (Article 9 of the Regulations on Operation and Review). Voting rights at the
   General Assembly are exercised by individual members in accordance with the
   Articles of Incorporation.

### Article 8 (Obligations of Branches, etc.)

Branches, etc. shall faithfully perform the following obligations.

1. **Compliance with the Articles of Incorporation and regulations:** Shall
   comply with the Society's Articles of Incorporation and these Regulations, as
   well as all detailed rules resolved by the Board of Directors.
2. **Activity reporting obligation:** Shall submit to the Society, by **January
   31** each year, the **annual business settlement report** for the previous
   year and the **business plan** for the current year.
3. **Realization of core values:** Shall mandatorily carry out activities to
   realize the core values of branch operation, namely **Publicity (public
   participation), Transparency, and Sustainability**.

## Chapter 4 Operation and Activities

### Article 9 (Election of the Branch Head and Officers)

1. The branch head shall, as a principle, be selected through election by the
   Full Members of the relevant branch or through a resolution of the Steering
   Committee. (The election method shall be determined by branch-specific
   rules.)
2. The term of office of the branch head and officers is **three years**, and
   they may be re-elected **only once**.

### Article 10 (Acting Duties and Handover)

1. In case of the sudden resignation of the branch head or a long-term absence
   of **three months or more**, an acting head shall be designated, in the order
   of the senior vice head of the branch and then the officers elected by the
   Steering Committee, to secure the continuity of organizational operations.
2. The acting head may perform duties only within the scope of the branch head's
   authority. The branch's officers shall, through a clear **handover
   procedure** covering administrative documents, financial ledgers, asset
   lists, etc., report the results to the Society.

### Article 11 (Society Approval of Major Projects)

1. Where a branch, etc. intends to hold a **major project that may create
   external risks**, such as a regional competition, a large-scale
   profit-generating business, or an overseas exchange exhibition, using the
   Society's name as a sponsoring name, it must obtain the **prior approval of
   the Society's Board of Directors**.
2. An application for approval shall be accompanied by risk management measures,
   including the business plan, budget proposal, and insurance plan.

### Article 12 (Obligation of Outreach Calligraphy)

To improve closeness to local communities, branches, etc. shall aim, as a
minimum target, to hold **"Outreach Calligraphy" (찾아가는 서예)** or pop-up
exhibitions at venues other than galleries **once or more per quarter (four
times a year)**.

## Chapter 5 Finance and Audit

### Article 13 (Financial Management and Accounting Principles)

1. The finances of branches, etc. shall, as a principle, be **independent
   accounts** separate from the Society's finances.
2. Where a branch, etc. has received **activity support funds** from the
   Society, such funds shall be managed in a separate account, and a
   **settlement report on use** shall be submitted in the format required by the
   Society.
3. To secure the financial sustainability of branch operations, a quantitative
   target shall be set such that **"revenue other than dues accounts for at
   least 10% of total annual revenue,"** and this target shall be resolved
   annually by the Steering Committee.

### Article 14 (Audit Authority and Procedures)

1. The Society has the authority to conduct **ad hoc audits** of the financial
   operations and major activities of branches, etc., on a regular basis or
   whenever deemed necessary.
2. The Society's audit shall review budget execution, financial ledgers, and key
   decision-making documents in accordance with Article 16, Paragraph ③ of the
   Articles of Incorporation, and branches, etc. shall cooperate faithfully.
3. Where an audit reveals fraud or a violation of financial regulations, the
   Society may proceed with the **recovery of activity support funds** and
   disciplinary procedures under Article 15.

## Chapter 6 Discipline and Dissolution

### Article 15 (Revocation of Accreditation and Discipline)

The Society's Board of Directors may resolve to revoke the accreditation of, or
suspend the activities of, the relevant branch, etc., or impose other
discipline, in any of the following cases.

1. **Shortfall in membership:** Where the minimum number of Full Members under
   Article 4 is not met continuously for one year or more
2. **Long-term suspension of activities:** Where, without justifiable reason,
   the core activities in the annual business plan are not carried out for **one
   year or more**, or the activity report is not submitted
3. **Damage to the Society's honor:** Where the Society's name is improperly
   used, causing legal disputes, or the Society's honor is significantly damaged
4. **Improper use of funds:** Where Society funds, such as activity support
   funds, are improperly used for purposes other than those intended

### Article 16 (Designation of Troubled Branches and Dispatch of Acting Administrators)

1. Where the Society's Board of Directors judges that normal operation is
   impossible due to disputes within a branch, a vacancy in the officer team, or
   a serious incident, the branch may be designated as a **troubled branch (사고
   지부)**.
2. For a troubled branch, the Society may temporarily dispatch an **acting
   administrator (직무 대행자)** to manage the organization and take measures to
   restore it to normal. In such case, the branch's existing officers shall
   follow the instructions of the acting administrator.
