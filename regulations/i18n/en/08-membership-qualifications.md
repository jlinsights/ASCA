# Qualifications and Requirements for Members and Officers

| Membership Category | Annual Dues | Remarks                                                |
| ------------------- | ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------ |
| Young Artist        | KRW 30,000  | Registration fee of KRW 10,000 upon initial membership |
| General Artist      | KRW 50,000  | Registration fee of KRW 20,000 upon initial membership |
| Recommended Artist  | KRW 80,000  | Registration fee of KRW 30,000 upon promotion          |
| Invited Artist      | KRW 100,000 | Registration fee of KRW 50,000 upon promotion          |
| Officer             | KRW 200,000 | Development fund paid upon assuming office             |

- The deadline for payment of annual dues is, as a principle, the end of January
  each year.
- The development fund upon assuming office may be paid in regular monthly
  installments.
- Young Artists are artists who are 40 years of age or younger as of January 1
  of the relevant year, and General Artists are artists who are 41 years of age
  or older as of the same date, in each case agreeing with the ideals and
  purposes of the Society.
- Recommended Artists are selected from Full Members who have been active for
  three years or more and have earned award points of 10 or more, and Invited
  Artists are selected from Recommended Artists who have been active for five
  years or more and have earned award points of 15 or more. For detailed
  criteria and procedures, see ⟪Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended
  and Invited Artists⟫.
- Officers are Invited Artists who satisfy the separate activity criteria
  required of officers (e.g., participation in five or more major Society events
  in the past three years, or service on specific committees), who have received
  a recommendation from the Board of Directors, and who have been elected by the
  General Assembly under Article 11 of the Articles of Incorporation.
- Full Members are Young Artists, General Artists, Recommended Artists, Invited
  Artists, and Officers who have paid annual dues or lifetime dues (Article 2,
  Paragraph 3 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues).

### **Qualifications of Members and Officers**

1. Members and officers of the Society are persons who are currently engaged in
   creative work, education, or research in traditional Hangul and Hanmun
   calligraphy, contemporary calligraphy, calligraphic lettering, wood and stone
   carving, seal carving, literati painting, ink-wash painting (梅, 蘭, 菊, 竹),
   folk painting, and similar fields, or who take an interest in volunteer
   service, such as sharing their talents with communities or individuals for
   research and development.
2. Persons who agree with the founding purpose of the Asian Society of
   Calligraphic Arts, have completed the prescribed membership procedure, and
   have paid the annual dues.
3. The qualifications of Recommended Artists, Invited Artists, officers, and
   others beyond the above are governed by the
   "[Articles of Incorporation](#Articles-of-Incorporation)" and the
   "[Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists](#Regulations-on-the-Appointment-of-Recommended-and-Invited-Artists)."
4. Recommended Artists require three years or more of Full Member experience and
   award records, and Invited Artists require five years or more of experience
   as Recommended Artists.

### **Rights and Obligations of Members and Officers**

1. Full Members of the Society have the right to vote and to be elected as
   officers in relation to exhibitions and other projects promoted by the
   Society, both domestically and overseas, and have the right to attend
   meetings, propose opinions on the Society's activities, and take part in
   resolutions.
2. Members may receive the Society's materials and publications free of charge.
   However, exhibition catalogs are governed by Article 9 of the Regulations on
   Membership and Dues (one free copy for Full Members who participate in an
   exhibition; additional copies are charged).
3. Members and officers must pay the prescribed entrance and registration fees
   and annual dues, and are obligated to comply with all matters resolved by the
   General Assembly.
4. Members and officers are obligated to submit works to exhibitions hosted by
   the Society, to attend various events, and to comply with the Articles of
   Incorporation and all related rules and regulations.
5. Where a member fails to pay dues and other charges under Article 7, Item 3 of
   the Articles of Incorporation, the restriction of rights, suspension of
   membership, and restoration are governed by Article 5 of the Regulations on
   Membership and Dues.
6. Payment of registration fees and annual dues is made through the
   [online donation page](https://secure.donus.org/orientalcalligraphy/pay), and
   members or officers may check their own payment history through
   [My Page](https://secure.donus.org/orientalcalligraphy/mypage).
7. All Full Members who pay annual dues, and officers who pay development funds,
   donations, and contributions at or above the amount set by the Society, will
   be provided with a cyberspace in which their career and works can be
   continuously managed and exhibited, and these will be posted on the Society's
   website.
