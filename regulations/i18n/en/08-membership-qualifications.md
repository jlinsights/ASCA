# Qualifications and Requirements for Artists, Members and Officers

This guide gathers in one place the qualifications and requirements for artists,
members and officers that are spread across the Articles of Incorporation and
the Society's Regulations. Where they differ, the original text of each
Regulation prevails, including the
[Articles of Incorporation](#Articles-of-Incorporation) and the
[Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists](#Regulations-on-the-Appointment-of-Recommended-and-Invited-Artists).

## Artists

1. Artists are classified as Young Artists, General Artists, Recommended Artists
   and Invited Artists. Young and General Artists are determined by age as of
   January 1 of the year; Recommended and Invited Artists are appointed after
   review.
2. Since 2024, certificates for Recommended and Invited Artists have been
   awarded separately in the Traditional and Contemporary categories
   (Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists, Article
   2(3)).
3. Artists who are not Full Members of the Society may also take part in solo
   and booth exhibitions within the Invited Artists' Exhibition (書境). The
   standard (non-member) rate under Article 7 of the Regulations on Membership
   and Dues applies to the entry fee, and further details follow the call for
   entries for that year.

### Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists

1. Recommended Artists are appointed from among those who have been active as
   Full Members for 3 years or more and have earned 10 or more award points;
   Invited Artists are appointed from among those who have been active as
   Recommended Artists for 5 years or more and have earned 15 or more award
   points (Appointment Regulations, Articles 2 and 9(2)).
2. Apart from the points, applicants must meet all of the following requirements
   (Appointment Regulations, Article 3): consecutive entries in the Society's
   exhibitions within the last 3 years, passing the Qualification Review
   Committee's review of a portfolio of representative works, 3 or more
   contribution activities for the Society, and no disciplinary action within
   the last 5 years.
3. Reviews are held twice a year as a document review and a review of works (3
   representative works made within the last 3 years). Qualification is granted
   with the approval of two-thirds or more of the jurors, and the Chairperson
   makes the final appointment (Appointment Regulations, Article 8).
4. A person who has made a special contribution to the development of
   calligraphy culture, a person who has worked in calligraphy education in
   Korea or abroad for 20 years or more, or an Invited Artist of another
   calligraphy organization registered with the Ministry of Culture, Sports and
   Tourism may be specially appointed by a resolution of two-thirds or more of
   the Board of Directors. The person must apply (or consent, if recommended by
   the Board), and the Board votes only on applicants who have passed the
   Qualification Review Committee's document and works review. For a person who
   is not a Full Member, the application also serves as the application for
   admission, and the special appointment registration fee under Article 3(4) of
   the Regulations on Membership and Dues applies (review fee, registration fee
   and first-year dues totalling KRW 180,000 for a Recommended Artist and KRW
   250,000 for an Invited Artist). Full Members pay the step-by-step promotion
   registration fees, and a suspended member must first pay all outstanding dues
   (Appointment Regulations, Article 11).
5. A person who has held Invited Artist status for 30 years or more, or a person
   aged 70 or over who has made a remarkable contribution to the Society's
   development, may be named an Honorary Artist. Honorary Artists are exempt
   from dues and entry obligations (Appointment Regulations, Article 12).

### Maintaining Recommended and Invited Artist Status

1. Recommended and Invited Artists must submit works for renewal every 2 years,
   take part in at least one Society exhibition a year, take part in guiding and
   mentoring emerging artists, and pay annual dues (Appointment Regulations,
   Article 9(1)).
2. Status is suspended for failing to take part in exhibitions for 2 consecutive
   years or for failing to pay annual dues for 2 years or more (Appointment
   Regulations, Article 10(1)).
3. Status is cancelled where the person has seriously damaged the Society's
   reputation, where a suspension has lasted more than 3 years, where the person
   has applied in writing to give up the status, or where the person has been
   expelled (Appointment Regulations, Article 10(2)).

## Members

1. A person who agrees with the founding purpose of the Society, submits an
   application for admission and obtains the approval of the Board of Directors
   becomes a member (Articles of Incorporation, Article 5).
2. Members are those who are currently engaged in creative work, education or
   research in traditional Hangeul and Hanja calligraphy, contemporary
   calligraphy, calligraphy design, wood carving, seal carving and literati
   painting, ink painting (plum, orchid, chrysanthemum and bamboo), folk
   painting and the like, or who are keenly interested in volunteer activities
   sharing their talents with communities or individuals, and who have completed
   the prescribed admission procedure and paid annual dues.
3. Members are divided into Annual Members, who pay annual dues every year, and
   Lifetime Members, who have paid lifetime dues and are permanently exempt from
   annual dues (Regulations on Membership and Dues, Article 2(1)).
4. Full Members are Young, General, Recommended and Invited Artists and Officers
   who have paid annual or lifetime dues. Members whose status is suspended, and
   the Associate, Special and Honorary Members under Article 6 of the Articles
   of Incorporation, are not Full Members (Regulations on Membership and Dues,
   Article 2(3)).
5. Full Members at the Invited Artist level or above may become Lifetime Members
   by paying lifetime dues equal to 10 times the annual dues, and are thereafter
   permanently exempt from annual dues (Regulations on Membership and Dues,
   Article 3(3)).
6. Associate, Special and Honorary Members may attend and speak at the General
   Assembly but have no voting rights (Articles of Incorporation, Article 6).
7. Members may withdraw freely by submitting a notice of withdrawal to the
   Chairperson (Articles of Incorporation, Article 8).

## Officers

1. Officers are Invited Artists who meet the separate activity standards
   required of officers (for example, taking part in 5 or more major Society
   events over the past 3 years, or committee activities), are recommended by
   the Board of Directors and are elected by the General Assembly (Regulations
   on Membership and Dues, Article 2(2); Articles of Incorporation, Article 11).
2. A member taking office as an Officer pays a development fund of KRW
   2,000,000, which may be paid in instalments by CMS over up to 20 months. On
   full payment the member automatically becomes a Lifetime Member; a member who
   is already a Lifetime Member pays the difference after deducting the lifetime
   dues already paid (Regulations on Membership and Dues, Articles 3(3) and 4).
3. Directors related to one another within the degree of kinship set out in
   Article 777 of the Civil Act may not exceed one-fifth of the number of
   Directors, and Auditors may not be related to one another or to any Director
   (Articles of Incorporation, Article 13).
4. An Officer who acts against the Society's purposes, engages in disputes among
   Officers, accounting fraud or markedly improper conduct, or obstructs the
   Society's work may be dismissed by a resolution of the General Assembly
   (Articles of Incorporation, Article 12).

## Payment of Dues and Status Management

1. As a principle, annual dues are paid by January 31 each year (Regulations on
   Membership and Dues, Article 5(1)).
2. If the current year's annual dues are unpaid, the powers needed for member
   activities are restricted; if dues are unpaid for 2 consecutive years,
   membership is suspended; and if dues are unpaid for 3 consecutive years, the
   Chairperson may expel the member by resolution of the Board of Directors
   under Article 9 of the Articles of Incorporation. Paying all outstanding dues
   restores the previous status, and an expelled member must apply for admission
   again (Regulations on Membership and Dues, Article 5).
3. Registration fees and annual dues are paid through the
   [online donation page](https://secure.donus.org/orientalcalligraphy/pay), and
   members or officers can check their own payment history on
   [My Page](https://secure.donus.org/orientalcalligraphy/mypage).

## Rights and Duties

1. Full Members of the Society have the right to vote and to stand for election
   with respect to the Society's projects, such as exhibitions in Korea and
   abroad, and its Officers, and have the right to attend meetings, propose
   opinions on the Society's activities and take part in resolutions.
2. Members may receive the Society's materials and publications free of charge.
   However, exhibition catalogs are governed by Article 9 of the Regulations on
   Membership and Dues (one free copy for Full Members taking part in the
   exhibition; additional copies are charged).
3. Members and Officers of the Society must pay the prescribed admission and
   registration fees and annual dues, and have the duty to comply with all
   matters resolved by the General Assembly.
4. Members and Officers of the Society have the duty to enter the exhibitions
   hosted by the Society, attend its various events, and comply with all
   regulations, including the Articles of Incorporation and related rules.
5. Members who have contributed to the Society's development may be rewarded,
   and members who act against the Society's purposes, damage its reputation or
   fail to fulfil their duties as members may be disciplined by expulsion,
   reprimand or the like (Articles of Incorporation, Article 9).
6. All Full Members who have paid annual dues, and Officers who have paid a
   development fund, sponsorship or donations of at least the amount set by the
   Society, are provided with an online space where they can continuously manage
   and exhibit their careers and works, which is posted on the Society's
   website.
