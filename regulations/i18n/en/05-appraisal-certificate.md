# Regulations on Artwork Appraisal and Issuance of Certificates of Authenticity

> [Effective 2026. 2. 13.] [No. 1, 2026. 2. 13., new enactment]

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 (Purpose)

These Regulations comply with the Art Promotion Act (「미술진흥법」) and related
laws. Their purpose is to set out the procedures and standards for
**authenticity appraisal, fair market value appraisal (valuation), and the
issuance of certificates** for works exhibited and sold at events organized by
the Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts (hereinafter the "Society"), thereby
**securing legal credibility**, providing transparency to the art market,
protecting the rights and interests of buyers, and **leading the establishment**
of a correct valuation of Korean calligraphy.

### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

These Regulations apply to calligraphy works traded at all exhibitions and sales
events planned, organized, or sponsored by the Society.

## Chapter 2 Appraisal Committee

### Article 3 (Composition of the Committee)

1. To ensure the fair appraisal of works, the Society establishes an
   **"Appraisal Committee" (hereinafter the "Committee")**.
2. The Committee consists of approximately five members, including
   **connoisseurship appraisers** well versed in calligraphic aesthetics and
   scripts, and **scientific appraisers** who are experts in conservation
   science and data analysis.
3. To maintain the expertise and fairness of appraisal, the Society shall
   clarify the qualification requirements for appraisal members and shall
   mandatorily conduct **regular advanced training and refresher training**.
4. Appraisal members must complete the expert appraiser training course
   organized by the Society and renew their qualifications.

## Chapter 3 Appraisal Standards and Procedures

### Article 4 (Principles of Appraisal)

Appraisal shall, as a principle, follow a **"multifaceted approach"** that
combines traditional connoisseurship, scientific analysis, and provenance
research.

### Article 5 (Methods of Appraisal)

The Committee shall determine authenticity by the following methods.

1. **Stylistic Analysis:** Compares and analyzes the artist's characteristic
   brushwork (用筆), structure, layout, seals (落款), and signatures against
   reference works.
2. **Scientific Analysis:** Where necessary, carries out compositional analysis
   of paper, ink, and seal paste (인주) (XRF, etc.) and dating tests to verify
   whether the materials are consistent with the period.
3. **AI-based Analysis:** Fine patterns and stroke characteristics that are
   difficult to identify with the naked eye may be supplementarily verified
   using AI algorithms.
4. **Provenance Research:** Traces exhibition catalogs, literature records,
   ownership history, etc., to confirm the movement history of the work.

## Chapter 4 Issuance and Management of Certificates

### Article 6 (Required Contents of Certificate)

A **Certificate of Authenticity** issued for a work determined to be genuine
must necessarily include the following.

1. Artist's name, title of the work, and year of creation
2. Materials and technique, and size of the work (height × width)
3. **High-resolution images of the whole work and of its details**
4. Declaration of Authenticity and the content of the guarantee
5. Information on the issuing organization, date of issue, and the signature and
   official seal of the representative appraiser
6. Appraised market value (Valuation Price) and the reference date of the
   valuation (only where the client has requested a market value appraisal)

### Article 7 (Unique Number and Prevention of Forgery)

1. All certificates shall be managed under a **unique identification number
   (Unique ID)** assigned by the Society to prevent alteration and forgery, and
   shall be permanently retained in the Society's database.
2. Certificate paper shall be **special paper with a watermark or anti-copy
   pattern**, and the Society's **embossed seal (철인)** shall be affixed over
   the image of the work to prevent physical alteration and forgery.

### Article 8 (Consent to Database Construction and Use)

1. High-resolution images of works and related appraisal data of works exhibited
   or donated to the Society and subjected to the appraisal procedure may be
   permanently used as non-commercial research data for the Society's
   "advancement of the AI authenticity appraisal algorithm" and "construction of
   a digital archive."
2. The exhibitor or donor of a work shall be **deemed to have explicitly
   consented** to the database construction and use under paragraph 1, and may
   not raise any objection.
3. The Society shall manage the data collected under paragraph 1 in a form that
   does not allow the identification of the artist or owner, and shall not
   disclose or use it externally for commercial purposes.

### Article 9 (Reissuance)

Certificates are, in principle, not reissued. However, where a certificate has
been damaged by unavoidable causes such as natural disasters, the owner may,
upon submitting evidence such as fragments of the original and after
deliberation by the Committee, have it reissued one time only.

- **Handling in case of loss:** In case of loss, it is reconfirmed that
  reissuance is **in principle not possible**. Where reissuance is exceptionally
  necessary, it shall be stipulated that reissuance may be made only through
  **stricter evidence**, such as the owner's written statement regarding the
  circumstances of the loss and a police report confirmation, and through
  **public notice procedures**. (A prescribed reissuance fee may also be set.)

### Article 10 (Scope of Guarantee and Exemption from Liability)

- **Clear limitation of liability:** The effect of the certificate is clearly
  limited to **"the authenticity at the time of issuance."**
- **Explicit exemption matters:** The guarantee shall lapse, or the Society may
  be exempted from liability, upon the occurrence of the following events.
  - Where the certificate is transferred without notifying the Society
  - Where the work has been damaged or altered
  - **Where the original certificate is not presented**
  - Where the result of an appraisal by a third-party institution differs,
    through the Society's re-review procedure, etc.
