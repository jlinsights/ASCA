# Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists

> [Effective 2023. 8. 23.] [No. 3, 2026. 1. 1., partial amendment]

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 (Purpose)

Pursuant to Article 41 of the Articles of Incorporation, the purpose of these
Regulations is to set out matters concerning the selection of Recommended
Artists and Invited Artists of the Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts
(hereinafter the "Society").

## Chapter 2 Qualification Review and Selection

### Article 2 (Method of Selection)

① Recommended Artists shall be selected from among those who obtain 10 points or
more by adding up the points set forth in the following items. ② Invited Artists
shall be selected from among those who obtain 15 points or more by adding up the
points set forth in the following items. ③ From 2024 onward, certificates of
Recommended Artist and Invited Artist shall be awarded separately for the
Traditional Division and the Contemporary Division.

### Article 3 (Essential Requirements for Qualification Review)

① Even a person who meets the point requirements of Article 2 shall be excluded
from appointment if they do not satisfy all of the following requirements.

1. A person with a record of continuous submissions to the Society's exhibitions
   within the three years preceding the date of application
2. A person who has passed the portfolio review of recent representative works
   conducted by the Qualification Review Committee
3. A person whose contributions to the Society, such as operating Society events
   or volunteer service, have been confirmed three or more times
4. A person who has not received any disciplinary action from the Society within
   the past five years

### Article 4 (Point Standards for Award Records)

① Points for winners of the Korea Oriental Calligraphy Grand Exhibition

1. Selected (入選): 1 point
2. Special Selection (特選): 3 points
3. Three-Script Prize (삼체상): 5 points
4. Five-Script Prize (오체상): 7 points
5. Excellence Prize (우수상): 7 points
6. Top Prize (최우수상): 8 points
7. Grand Prize (대상): 9 points

② Award records from the international calligraphy exhibitions hosted by the
Society shall be given the same points as in paragraph ①. ③ Where a winning work
has been included in an album (화첩), 0.5 points shall be additionally added. ④
Where a winning work that received the Grand Prize is held in the collection of
a national museum or art museum, 1 point shall be additionally added. ⑤ For
points earned in the same year, only the highest score is recognized.

### Article 5 (Point Standards for Branch and Chapter Exhibitions)

① Award records from exhibitions hosted by the Society's branches and chapters
shall be given differentiated points as follows.

1. Metropolitan city or province level: 2 points
2. City, county, or district level: 1 point ② An additional 1 point shall be
   added for award records held for three consecutive years. ③ Where points have
   been earned from the Korea Oriental Calligraphy Grand Exhibition or an
   exhibition of another chapter in the same year, only the highest score is
   recognized.

### Article 6 (Bonus Points for Education, Career, and Contribution)

① Bonus points for calligraphy-related education are granted as follows.

1. Holders of a bachelor's degree or completers of a calligraphy-related
   department: 2 points
2. Holders of a master's degree or completers of a calligraphy-related
   department: an additional 1 point
3. Holders of a doctoral degree or completers of a calligraphy-related
   department: an additional 2 points

② Bonus points are granted for the following careers.

1. Holders of calligraphy-related certifications: 1 point
2. Successors of traditional calligraphy: persons with a record of at least
   three years of apprenticeship under a calligraphy master who holds at least
   the qualification of an Invited Artist of the Society: 2 points
3. Holders of overseas calligraphy-related degrees: recognized at the equivalent
   degree level

③ Bonus points (up to 3 points) are granted to those who have contributed to the
development of the Society and the spread of social value.

1. Persons who have served three or more times as an operating or executive
   committee member or volunteer for a Society event: 1 point
2. Persons whose planning activities for the activation of Society branches or
   chapters are documented: 1 point
3. Persons whose social contribution (ESG) activities, including the donation of
   calligraphy talent, are certified at 20 hours or more per year: 1 point
4. Young Artists (Article 2, Paragraph ② of the Regulations on Membership and
   Dues) who have carried out online promotion group activities for at least one
   year: 1 point

## Chapter 3 Review Procedures

### Article 7 (Qualification Review Committee)

① The Qualification Review Committee consists of the following members.

1. Five or more Invited Artists
2. **Three or more external experts (provided that external experts account for
   at least one-third of all members)**
3. Members appointed by the Board of Directors ② The term of members is two
   years, and members may be re-appointed. ③ To ensure the fairness of the
   review, a majority of the members shall be newly appointed.

### Article 8 (Review Method)

① Reviews are conducted twice a year and are divided into document review and
work review.

② The work review targets three representative works produced within the three
years preceding the review.

③ Qualification is granted with the approval of two-thirds or more of all
members of the review committee.

④ Applicants shall complete and submit the separate form
"[**Recommended and Invited Artist Application Form**](https://orientalcalligraphy.org/forms#request-for-invited)."
The Chairperson makes the final appointment after deliberation by the
Qualification Review Committee.

## Chapter 4 Qualification Management

### Article 9 (Obligations for Maintaining Qualification)

① Recommended Artists and Invited Artists shall fulfill the following
obligations.

1. Submission of works for qualification renewal every two years
2. Participation in exhibitions hosted by the Society at least once a year
3. Participation in guidance and mentoring of emerging artists
4. Payment of the prescribed annual dues

② The qualification requirements for Recommended Artists and Invited Artists are
as follows.

1. A Recommended Artist must have been an active Full Member of the Society for
   three years or more and must have award records in a nationwide competition
   recognized by the Society.
2. An Invited Artist must have at least five years of experience as a
   Recommended Artist of the Society.
3. Persons who meet all the qualification requirements in the preceding items
   shall be finally appointed by the Chairperson after deliberation by the
   Qualification Review Committee under Article 8.

### Article 10 (Suspension and Revocation of Qualification)

① Qualification shall be suspended in any of the following cases.

1. Non-attendance at exhibitions for two consecutive years
2. Non-payment of annual dues for two years or more

② Qualification shall be revoked in any of the following cases.

1. Where the Society's honor has been seriously damaged: where, through the acts
   listed in the following sub-items, it is judged by a resolution of the Board
   of Directors under Article 9 of the Articles of Incorporation that
   maintaining qualification is difficult

a. Violation of laws resulting in a sentence of imprisonment or a heavier
penalty, or causing a serious social controversy

b. Confirmed plagiarism or dishonest conduct in works

c. Causing serious damage to the Society or other members, or obstructing the
Society's activities through continuous defamation, dissemination of false
facts, etc. 2. Where the suspension period exceeds three years 3. Where the
person has applied in writing to renounce qualification 4. Where the person has
been expelled under Article 5 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues

## Chapter 5 Special Provisions

### Article 11 (Special Appointment)

① Any of the following persons may be specially appointed upon approval by
two-thirds or more of the Board of Directors.

1. Persons who have made a special contribution to the development of
   calligraphic culture
2. Persons who have been engaged in calligraphy education in Korea or abroad for
   20 years or more
3. Invited Artists of other calligraphy organizations registered with the
   Ministry of Culture, Sports and Tourism

② A special appointment may be made only upon the person's own application.
Where the Board of Directors recommends a candidate, the candidate is deemed to
have applied once the candidate consents. The application shall state the status
applied for (Recommended Artist or Invited Artist) and the category (Traditional
or Contemporary).

③ An applicant shall undergo the document review and review of works conducted
by the Qualification Review Committee under Article 7 in accordance with Article
8, Paragraphs 2 and 3, at the regular review under Article 8, Paragraph 1, and
the Board of Directors shall pass the resolution under Paragraph 1 only for an
applicant who has passed the Committee's review. The Board may resolve on the
status applied for or a lower status, and once resolved, the Chairperson makes
the appointment. In this case, Article 2, Article 3, Paragraph 1, Items 1 and 3,
and Article 9, Paragraph 2 do not apply, while Article 3, Paragraph 1, Items 2
and 4 apply.

④ Where a person who is not a Full Member of the Society applies, the following
apply.

1. The application also serves as the application for admission under Article 5
   of the Articles of Incorporation, and the Board's resolution under Paragraph
   1 also serves as approval of admission under that Article.
2. The applicant pays the special appointment registration fee under Article 3,
   Paragraph 4 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues instead of the
   registration fee under Paragraph 2 of that Article. The review fee is paid
   upon application and is not refunded regardless of the outcome; the
   registration fee and the annual dues for the year of appointment are paid
   together after the appointment is resolved, based on the status resolved.
3. The appointment takes effect, and the person becomes a Full Member of that
   grade, on the day the registration fee and the annual dues for the year of
   appointment are paid (or on the day of the Board's resolution where both are
   fully waived under Paragraph 7). The annual dues for the year of appointment
   are deemed paid (including where waived under Paragraph 7), and from the
   following year annual dues are paid under Article 3, Paragraph 1 and Article
   5 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues.

⑤ Where a Full Member of the Society applies, the member pays, after the
appointment is resolved, the sum of the promotion registration fees under
Article 3, Paragraph 2 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues for each step
from the current grade to the grade resolved (for example, KRW 30,000 plus KRW
50,000, or KRW 80,000, for appointment from General Artist to Invited Artist),
and the appointment takes effect on the day of payment (or on the day of the
Board's resolution where waived under Paragraph 7). Annual dues already paid,
and the exemption from annual dues of a Lifetime Member under Paragraph 3 of
that Article, remain unaffected.

⑥ A member whose powers are restricted or whose status is suspended under
Article 5 of the Regulations on Membership and Dues may apply only after
restoring that status by paying all outstanding annual dues, in which case
Paragraph 5 applies.

⑦ For a person falling under Paragraph 1, Item 1, the Board of Directors may,
when resolving on the appointment, reduce or waive in whole or in part the
registration fee and the annual dues for the year of appointment under Paragraph
4, or the promotion registration fees under Paragraph 5. The review fee is not
reduced.

⑧ A specially appointed person bears the obligations under Article 9, Paragraph
1 from the day the appointment takes effect.

### Article 12 (Honorary Artist)

① Any of the following persons may be nominated as an Honorary Artist.

1. Persons who have maintained qualification as an Invited Artist for 30 years
   or more
2. Persons aged 70 or older who have made a significant contribution to the
   development of the Society ② Honorary Artists are exempt from the obligations
   of dues and exhibition submission.

### Article 13 (International Exchange)

① To promote international calligraphy exchange, the following are recognized.

1. Award records from international calligraphy exhibitions
2. Records of participation in exchange exhibitions with overseas calligraphy
   organizations ② The Society may operate a mutual certification system with
   overseas calligraphy organizations.
