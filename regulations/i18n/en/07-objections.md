# Regulations on the Handling of Objections

> [Effective 2026. 2. 13.] [No. 1, 2026. 2. 13., new enactment]

### Article 1 (Purpose)

The purpose of these Regulations is to establish procedural justice by handling,
fairly and promptly, objections that may arise in all competition and exhibition
procedures organized by the Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts (hereinafter the
"Society").

### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

These Regulations apply to all competitions and exhibition projects that the
Society hosts, organizes, or operates under entrustment.

### Article 3 (Filing Period)

1. An objection must be filed within **seven days** from the date of the
   official announcement of the results.
2. Where a separate deadline is specified in the call for entries (공모요강) of
   an individual project, that deadline shall apply first.

### Article 4 (Filing Method and Required Items)

An objection shall, as a principle, be filed in writing (by email or through the
designated form), and must necessarily include the following.

- **Personal information:** Name, contact information, reception number (or
  entry division)
- **Subject matter:** The specific item or procedure to which the objection is
  raised
- **Grounds and basis:** Specific reasons based on objective facts (submission
  of evidence is recommended)
- **Requests:** Specific requests, such as correction, confirmation of facts, or
  re-review

**Filing channels**

- **Email:** info@orientalcalligraphy.org
- **Telephone:** 0502-5550-8700 (Even when filed by telephone, the required
  items above must be submitted in writing.)

### Article 5 (Processing Principles and Deadlines)

1. **Acknowledgment of receipt:** A notice of receipt is sent within 24 business
   hours of filing.
2. **First response:** The progress of the review is shared **within three
   business days** from the date of filing.
3. **Final response:** The final decision is officially notified **within 14
   business days** from the date of filing. (However, where specialized
   re-verification is required, the period may be extended, and the reason for
   the delay shall be notified in advance.)

### Article 6 (Processing Standards by Type)

1. **Procedural errors:** Administrative mistakes in reception, omission, or
   announcement shall be checked immediately and corrected.
2. **Factual errors:** Errors in entrant information or work information shall
   be corrected after verification of evidence.
3. **Verification requests:** Where a request is made for verification of
   on-site calligraphy results or comparison with original works,
   re-verification may be conducted under a resolution of the Judging Panel or
   the Steering Committee.

### Article 7 (Dismissal and Rejection)

The Society may decline to accept an objection in the following cases.

- Where the prescribed filing period has passed
- Where required information is missing, making review impossible
- Where the objection is a mere complaint about a Judge's subjective evaluation
  (score)
- Where there are baseless slander, personal attacks, or a clear purpose of
  obstructing business
