# Regulations on Exhibition Management and Operation

> [Effective 2026. 1. 1.] [No. 1, 2026. 1. 1., new enactment]

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 (Purpose)

The purpose of these Regulations is to set out matters concerning the planning,
promotion, artwork sales, and overall operation of exhibitions organized or
agency-handled by the Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts (hereinafter the
"Society"), thereby enhancing the efficiency of exhibition business, protecting
the rights and interests of members, and supporting creative activities.

### Article 2 (Scope of Application)

These Regulations apply to all exhibition-related profit-generating businesses
and agency services that the Society hosts, organizes, or sponsors, or performs
upon entrustment from members (or outside artists). However, matters concerning
the judging and operation of the Korea Oriental Calligraphy Grand Exhibition
shall follow separate regulations.

## Chapter 2 Exhibition Agency Services and Planning Support

### Article 3 (Scope of Agency Services)

To support the holding of solo exhibitions by applicant artists, the Society may
agency-handle the following services.

1. Booking of exhibition venues and handling of administrative procedures
2. Exhibition planning, staging, and production assistance
3. Online and offline promotion and marketing
4. Other matters necessary for holding the exhibition

### Article 4 (Cost Burden and Sponsorship)

① Venue rental fees and other expenses for agency-handled exhibitions (catalog
production, transport, installation, dismantling, etc.) shall, following the
principle that the beneficiary bears the cost, be **fully borne by the
participating artist**.

② However, the Chairperson may, taking into account the artist's capabilities,
the artist's contribution to the Society, and the Society's financial situation,
**ex officio provide a prescribed sponsorship or reduce or waive the agency
fee**.

③ The artist shall deposit the confirmed expenses into the account designated by
the Society no later than 10 days before the venue rental payment deadline.

### Article 4-2 (Discovery of Emerging Artists and Support for Artistic Activities)

① To discover young and emerging artists and to support their registration of
artistic activity certification (예술인 등록), the Society may operate **planned
exhibitions (such as incubating exhibitions)** hosted by the Society.

② To reduce the cost burden on participating artists, such planned exhibitions
shall be conducted in the form of joint exhibitions (booth exhibitions or group
exhibitions). Each artist shall be allocated an independent exhibition space and
catalog space so that their artistic activity records can be recognized.

③ The participation fee shall be determined by a resolution of the Board of
Directors, taking into account actual costs such as venue rental, catalog
production, and publicity.

④ To enable participating artists to smoothly register artistic activity
certification with the Korea Arts Welfare Foundation (한국예술인복지재단), the
Society provides administrative conveniences such as the execution of standard
contracts and the issuance of certificates of achievement.

## Chapter 3 Exhibition Promotion Agency Services

### Article 5 (Promotion Agency Services)

① To effectively promote the works of exhibiting artists, the Society provides
the following services for a fee.

1. Posting on the Society's official website and SNS channels
2. Dispatch of text messages to members and publication in newsletters
3. Agency for registering information on major portal sites and art communities

② **The fee for promotion agency services is, as a principle, KRW 330,000 per
month (inclusive of value-added tax)**, but it may be adjusted by a resolution
of the Board of Directors in response to price increases and the scope of
services.

### Article 6 (Application and Payment)

A person wishing to use the promotion agency service shall complete and submit
the prescribed application form, and pay the full fee no later than seven days
before the service begins.

## Chapter 4 Artwork Sales and Settlement

### Article 7 (Sales Commission and Settlement)

① When works are sold during an exhibition organized or agency-handled by the
Society, the sale price shall be based on the price set by the artist.

② The Society shall collect **30% of the final sale price** as a commission to
cover card payment fees and other administrative costs incurred in the sales
agency.

③ The remaining **70%**, after deduction of the commission, shall be paid to the
artist within 14 days from the day the exhibition closes, **after the sale
payment has been completed and the buyer's purchase has been confirmed (the
7-day return period from the date of purchase has passed)**.

④ Taxes and public charges arising in connection with sales (such as income tax)
shall be handled in accordance with applicable laws.

⑤ If a work is returned within the return period for a reason other than a
defect, i.e., a change of mind, the Society shall refund the sale price to the
buyer and shall not collect the commission on that sale. Administrative costs
already incurred, such as card payment fees, shall be borne by the buyer. Works
for which settlement has been made to the artist after the return period has
passed shall not be accepted for return due to a change of mind.

## Chapter 5 Miscellaneous Provisions

### Article 8 (Issuance of Certificates of Achievement)

① When an artist who has participated in an exhibition organized or
agency-handled by the Society requests proof of achievement to apply for
government or local government artistic support programs (such as the Artist
Pass or creative preparation grants), the Society shall issue a **[Certificate
of Artistic Activity Achievement]**.

② The issuance fee for the certificate under paragraph ① is free of charge for
members. For non-members, a prescribed issuance fee may be charged.

### Article 9 (Contract Termination and Refund)

① When an artist wishes to cancel an exhibition agency or promotion agency
contract for personal reasons, a refund shall be made according to the following
criteria.

1. Up to 7 days before the start of the service (posting of promotional
   materials, payment of venue rental fees, etc.): full refund
2. From 7 days to 1 day before the start of the service: 50% refund of the total
   cost
3. After the start of the service: no refund

② Costs already incurred (such as the venue booking deposit and design costs)
are excluded from the refund amount and settled at actual cost.

### Article 10 (Damages)

If, during an exhibition, the artist causes damage to the Society or a third
party through reasons attributable to the artist, the artist shall compensate
for such damage.

### Article 11 (Miscellaneous)

Matters not specified in these Regulations shall follow a resolution of the
Board of Directors or general custom.

## Chapter 6 Public Accessibility and Educational Programs

### Article 12 (Digital Archive Construction and Public Access)

① The Society shall digitize past award-winning works and members' works to
build and operate an online calligraphy archive.

② After applying copyright protection technology, the archive materials shall be
opened for free viewing by the general public, limited to non-profit educational
and research purposes.

### Article 13 (Public Calligraphy Education and Experience)

① To popularize calligraphy, the Society shall open an "Open Calligraphy
Academy" or free experience classes for the general public at least twice a
year.

② To encourage participation by youth and university students, the Society may
establish a separate youth calligraphy competition track and reduce
participation fees.

## Addenda

## Addenda <No. 1, 2026. 01. 01>

### Article 1 (Effective Date)

These Regulations take effect from the date of promulgation.

### Article 2 (Transitional Provision)

Exhibition agency contracts concluded before the enforcement of these
Regulations shall follow prior practice.
