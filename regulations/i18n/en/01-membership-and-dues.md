# Regulations on Membership and Dues

> [Effective 2026. 1. 1.]

### Article 1 (Purpose)

These Regulations clearly set out the membership qualifications, dues structure,
payment procedures, and related matters of the Asian Society of Calligraphic
Arts (ASCA) (hereinafter the "Society"), in order to ensure the sound financial
management of the Society and equity among members, and to contribute to the
development of Oriental calligraphy culture.

### Article 2 (Types and Definitions of Members)

① **(Classification of Members)** The members of the Society are classified,
according to their payment obligations, as "Annual Members" and "Lifetime
Members," as follows.

**a. Annual Member**: A member who joins the Society and pays the annual dues
specified in Article 3 of these Regulations every year

**b. Lifetime Member**: A member who joins the Society and, having paid the
lifetime dues specified in **Article 3, Paragraph 3 or Article 4** of these
Regulations in a lump sum (or in installments), is permanently exempted from the
obligation to pay annual dues

② **(Grades of Members)** Members under paragraph ① are granted the following
grades according to their activity record and achievements, and the rights and
obligations for each grade are separately determined by the Board of Directors.

a. "**Young Artist**": An artist who is **40 years of age or younger** as of
January 1 of the relevant year and who agrees with the ideals and purposes of
the Society

b. "**General Artist**": An artist who is **41 years of age or older** as of
January 1 of the relevant year and who agrees with the ideals and purposes of
the Society

c. "**Recommended Artist**": A person appointed as a Recommended Artist under
the ⟪Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists⟫

d. "**Invited Artist**": A person appointed as an Invited Artist under the
⟪Regulations on the Appointment of Recommended and Invited Artists⟫

e. "**Officer**": An Invited Artist who **satisfies the separate activity
criteria required of officers (e.g., participation in five or more major Society
events in the past three years, or service on specific committees), has received
a recommendation from the Board of Directors, and has been elected by the
General Assembly under Article 11 of the Articles of Incorporation**

③ **(Full Members)** Under these Regulations and other regulations, "Full
Members" means Young Artists, General Artists, Recommended Artists, Invited
Artists, and Officers who have paid the annual dues or lifetime dues under
Article 3. Members whose qualifications have been suspended under Article 5, and
Associate, Special, and Honorary Members under Article 6 of the Articles of
Incorporation, are not included in Full Members.

**b. (Dues)** Lifetime dues are set at an amount equal to ten times the annual
dues (Paragraph ①).

**c. (Special Provision for Officers)** An officer who has paid a development
fund of KRW 2,000,000 under Article 4 is deemed to have fully paid the lifetime
dues and is automatically granted Lifetime Member status.

**d. (Benefits and Maintenance of Status)** A member who has fully paid the
lifetime dues permanently retains Lifetime Member status regardless of whether
the member later assumes office, and is permanently exempted from the obligation
to pay annual dues (Paragraph ①) thereafter.

**e. (Obligation upon Assuming Office)** The development fund for a Lifetime
Member who assumes office shall be as provided in Article 4, Paragraph 4.

### Article 4 (Officer Development Fund and Benefits)

① A member who assumes office as an officer shall pay a **development fund of
KRW 2,000,000** for the development of the Society.

② The development fund may be paid in installments over up to 20 months through
CMS (automatic bank transfer).

③ A Full Member who has fully paid the development fund of KRW 2,000,000 under
paragraph ① **automatically acquires Lifetime Member status and is permanently
exempted from the obligation to pay annual dues thereafter.**

④ Where a member who has already acquired Lifetime Member status under Article
3, Paragraph 3 is appointed as an officer, the member shall pay **the difference
obtained by deducting the lifetime dues already paid** from the development fund
of KRW 2,000,000 under paragraph ①.

### Article 5 (Dues Payment Period and Membership Management)

① As a rule, annual dues shall be paid by January 31 of each year.

② Measures for non-payment of dues

**a. First non-payment (non-payment of the current year's annual dues):** After
notice of non-payment is given, the member's rights necessary for membership
activities (e.g., access to web pages, participation in certain events) are
restricted.

**b. Second non-payment (non-payment for two consecutive years):** Membership is
suspended, and all member benefits and rights are discontinued.

**c. Third non-payment (non-payment for three consecutive years):** Under
Article 9 of the Articles of Incorporation, the Chairperson may expel the member
upon a resolution of the Board of Directors.

③ A member whose rights have been restricted or whose membership has been
suspended shall have the former status restored upon full payment of the
outstanding annual dues. A member who has been expelled must re-apply for
membership under Article 5 of the Articles of Incorporation.

④ In special circumstances, payment may be deferred or made in installments with
the approval of the Board of Directors.

### Article 8 (Mounting Fee Regulations)

① A mounting fee is charged for works awarded Selected (입선) or above, and
mounting shall be carried out in the **standard options and prices set by the
Society**.

② The mounting service provided by the Society shall, as a principle, follow the
**hand-backing method (손배접)** rather than the machine-backing method
(기계배접).

③ The mounting fee is calculated **based on the actual cost of mounting**, and
Full Members are offered a discount.

④ The mounting fee by work size is as follows.

| Work Size                                     | Standard Rate (applies to non-members) | Member Rate (applies to Full Members) |

### Article 10 (Individual Artist Homepage Initial Setup and Annual Maintenance Fee Regulations)

① A Full Member who has concluded a homepage production and maintenance contract
and **has paid the annual maintenance fee** shall be exempted from the annual
dues for that year.

② The following benefits are provided to artists who have exhibited **40 or more
works** in exhibitions.

**a. (Initial Benefit)** The homepage production fee and the first-year
maintenance fee are fully waived, and the annual dues for that year are also
waived.

**b. (Continuing Benefit)** From the second year onward, by paying **only the
annual maintenance fee**, the artist continues to receive homepage maintenance
service, and **the annual dues for that year are waived**.

③ For artists who have exhibited **30 or more works**, the initial setup fee is
waived, and the annual dues for that year are waived.

④ For artists who have exhibited **20 or more works**, the initial setup fee is
discounted by 50%.

### Article 11 (Payment Methods)

① All fees shall, as a rule, be paid online (bank transfer, CMS direct debit,
etc.).

② Payment shall be made through the online payment system, and an electronic
receipt must be issued if the purchaser so requests.

### Article 12 (Basic Benefits of Full Members)

**① Common Benefits for Full Members**

a. One catalog provided free of charge when participating in an exhibition

b. Discounted rates applied to entry fees, mounting fees, and catalog fees

c. Annual dues waived when building an individual artist homepage

**② Additional Benefits for Recommended Artists and Above**

a. Opportunity to register as a candidate for Invited Artist

b. Priority in participation in special exhibitions and international exchange
exhibitions

c. Selective support for initial homepage setup costs when requirements are met

**③ Additional Benefits for Invited Artists and Above**

a. Eligibility to recommend candidates for Officer (elected by the General
Assembly under Article 11 of the Articles of Incorporation)

b. Eligibility as a Steering Committee Member or Juror (Article 3 of the
Regulations on Operation and Review)

**④ Officer Benefits**

a. Priority in appointment as a Steering Committee Member or Juror

b. One-year waiver of the initial homepage setup fee and annual maintenance fee

c. 5% discount on the entry fee for the Invited Artists' Exhibition (the amount
set in the call for entries of that exhibition)

### Article 13 (Benefits for Senior Members)

Members aged 70 or older with 10 years or more of membership, whose contribution
to the development of the Society is recognized as significant under the
following criteria, may be granted an exemption from annual dues upon
deliberation by the Board of Directors.

① Participation in five or more major Society exhibitions

② Three or more lectures at Society seminars or workshops

③ Playing a key role in planning and carrying out Society projects, etc.

In such case, the merit deliberation shall be conducted objectively in
accordance with separately prepared "Merit Review Criteria," and where
necessary, a "Merit Review Subcommittee" may be formed to conduct the
evaluation.

### Article 14 (Branch Activity Support Fund)

① Each year, within the scope of the budget, the Society shall include a "Branch
Activity Support Fund" for the activation of branches and chapters.

② A regional competition or planned exhibition hosted by a branch may use the
"Society sponsorship name" upon approval by the Board of Directors of the
Society, and the Society shall support the necessary publicity and the dispatch
of judges for it.

③ Branches with outstanding activities may be commended at the year-end General
Assembly, and the support fund for the following year may be increased.

## Addenda <2026. 1. 1.>

### Article 1 (Effective Date)

These Regulations take effect from the date of promulgation following a
resolution of the Board of Directors.

### Article 2 (Application Mutatis Mutandis)

Matters not specified in these Regulations shall be as resolved by the Board of
Directors.

### Article 3 (Amendment)

These Regulations shall be amended by a resolution of the Board of Directors
under Article 41 of the Articles of Incorporation, and the amendment shall be
reported to the next General Assembly.
