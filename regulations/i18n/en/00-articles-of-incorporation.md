# Articles of Incorporation

## Chapter 1 General Provisions

### Article 1 (Name)

This corporation is named "Asian Society of Calligraphic Arts (ASCA)"
(hereinafter the "Society").

### Article 2 (Location)

① The principal office of the Society shall be located in Seoul Special City.

② The branch offices of the Society (Branches) shall be 1. Gwangju Branch 2.
Cheongju Branch 3. Chungju Branch, and Chapters and Branches shall be
established in other necessary localities (each si and do).

### Article 3 (Purpose)

The Society aims to establish the original and independent character of Korean
calligraphy circles; to promote friendship with the countries of the
Han-character cultural sphere (Korea, China, and Japan), which form the
foundation of Oriental script arts; to develop scripts, including Hangul, and
elevate them into an art of the world; to pioneer a new history of calligraphy
in the 21st century; and to contribute to art education and the cultivation of
the calligraphic spirit.

### Article 4 (Activities)

To achieve the purpose of the preceding Article, the Society shall carry out the
following businesses.

1. Holding Korea-China-Japan joint exhibitions and discovering new artists
   through nationwide open competitions
2. International and domestic exchange exhibitions and academic presentations to
   introduce calligraphy culture
3. Development and fostering of Korean-style scripts
4. Research and presentation of overseas calligraphy literature
5. Other businesses necessary to achieve the purpose of the Society

## Chapter 2 Members

### Article 5 (Qualifications of Members)

Members of the Society are persons who agree with the founding purpose of the
Society, have submitted the prescribed application for membership, and have
obtained the approval of the Board of Directors.

### Article 6 (Rights of Members)

Members have the right to take part in the operation of the Society through the
General Assembly. However, Associate Members, Special Members, and Honorary
Members may attend the General Assembly and speak, but have no voting rights.

### Article 7 (Obligations of Members)

Members shall have the following obligations.

1. Compliance with the Articles of Incorporation and all regulations of the
   Society
2. Implementation of resolutions of the General Assembly and the Board of
   Directors
3. Payment of membership dues and other charges

### Article 8 (Withdrawal of Members)

A member may freely withdraw from the Society by submitting a written withdrawal
notice to the Chairperson.

### Article 9 (Commendation and Discipline of Members)

① A member who has contributed to the development of the Society may be
commended upon a resolution of the Board of Directors.

② A member who engages in conduct contrary to the purpose of the Society or
conduct that damages its honor and prestige, or who fails to perform the
obligations under Article 7, may be disciplined, such as by expulsion or
censure, by the Chairperson upon a resolution of the Board of Directors or the
General Assembly.

## Chapter 3 Officers

### Article 10 (Types and Number of Officers)

The Society shall have the following officers.

1. Chairperson: 1 person
2. Directors: 15 persons (including the Chairperson)
3. Auditors: 2 persons

※ The number of Directors shall be set at no fewer than five persons.

### Article 11 (Election of Officers)

① Officers shall be elected by the General Assembly, and the Society shall
report their assumption of office to the competent authority without delay.

② A by-election to fill a vacancy shall be held within two months from the date
the vacancy occurs.

③ The election of new officers shall be held no later than two months before the
expiration of the term.

### Article 12 (Dismissal of Officers)

An officer who commits any of the following acts may be dismissed upon a
resolution of the General Assembly.

1. Conduct contrary to the purpose of the Society
2. Disputes among officers, accounting irregularities, or other significant
   improper conduct
3. Conduct that obstructs the business of the Society

### Article 13 (Restrictions on Election of Officers)

① In electing officers, the Directors may not include more than one-fifth of the
prescribed number of Directors who are relatives within the meaning of Article
777 of the Civil Act of one another.

② Auditors may not be relatives of one another, nor relatives of any Director
within the meaning of Article 777 of the Civil Act.

### Article 14 (Standing Directors)

① The Society may appoint Standing Directors to exclusively handle the business
of the Society's purposes.

② Standing Directors shall be appointed by the Chairperson from among the
Directors upon a resolution of the Board of Directors.

### Article 15 (Term of Officers)

① The term of officers is four years.

② The term of an officer who assumes office by by-election is the remaining term
of the predecessor.

### Article 16 (Duties of Officers)

① The Chairperson represents the Society, supervises its business, and serves as
chair of the General Assembly and the Board of Directors.

② Directors attend meetings of the Board of Directors, resolve matters
concerning the business of the Society, and handle matters entrusted by the
Board or the Chairperson.

③ Auditors perform the following duties.

1. Auditing the property status of the Society
2. Auditing the operation of the General Assembly and the Board of Directors and
   matters concerning their business
3. Where the audit results under items 1 and 2 reveal irregularities or improper
   conduct, demanding correction from the Board of Directors or the General
   Assembly and reporting to the competent authority
4. Where necessary to make the correction demand and report under item 3,
   demanding the convocation of the General Assembly or the Board of Directors
5. Stating opinions to the General Assembly, the Board of Directors, or the
   Chairperson regarding the property status and business of the Society

### Article 17 (Acting Chairperson)

① When the Chairperson is unable to serve, a Director designated by the
Chairperson shall act on behalf of the Chairperson.

② When the office of Chairperson is vacant, the Directors shall act on behalf of
the Chairperson in order of seniority.

③ A Director acting on behalf of the Chairperson under paragraph ② shall without
delay begin the procedure for electing a Chairperson.

## Chapter 4 General Assembly

### Article 18 (Composition of the General Assembly)

The General Assembly is the highest decision-making body of the Society and
consists of its members.

### Article 19 (Classification and Convocation)

① The General Assembly is divided into a Regular General Assembly and an
Extraordinary General Assembly, and is convened by the Chairperson.

② A Regular General Assembly shall be convened no later than one month before
the start of each fiscal year, and an Extraordinary General Assembly shall be
convened when the Chairperson deems it necessary.

③ The Chairperson shall notify each member in writing of the convocation of the
General Assembly, specifying the agenda, date, and place, no later than seven
days before the meeting begins.

### Article 20 (Special Provisions on Convocation of the General Assembly)

① The Chairperson shall convene the General Assembly within 14 days from the
date of any of the following demands for convocation.

1. A demand by a majority of the Directors in office, stating the purpose of the
   meeting
2. A demand by an Auditor under Article 16, Paragraph ③, item 4
3. A demand by one-fifth or more of the members on the roll, stating the purpose
   of the meeting

② If the person authorized to convene the General Assembly is vacant or evades
convening it, making convocation impossible for seven days or more, the General
Assembly may be convened with the approval of a majority of the Directors in
office or one-third or more of the members on the roll.

③ The chair of a General Assembly convened under paragraph ② shall be elected
from among the attending Directors, with the eldest presiding.

### Article 21 (Quorum and Voting)

① The General Assembly is opened upon the attendance of a majority of the
members on the roll, and resolutions are adopted by the approval of a majority
of the attending members.

② A member may delegate their voting right in writing to another member
attending the General Assembly. In such case, the proxy shall be submitted to
the chair before the General Assembly begins.

### Article 22 (Functions of the General Assembly)

The General Assembly resolves the following matters.

1. Election and dismissal of officers
2. Election and dismissal of officers
3. Disposal and acquisition of basic property, and borrowing of funds
4. Approval of the budget and settlement of accounts
5. Approval of business plans
6. Other important matters

### Article 23 (Grounds for Disqualification from General Assembly Resolutions)

A member shall not take part in a resolution when any of the following applies.

1. When resolving matters concerning the member's own election or dismissal as
   an officer
2. When the matter concerns money or property, litigation, or similar matters in
   which the member's interests conflict with those of the Society

## Chapter 5 Board of Directors

### Article 24 (Composition of the Board of Directors)

The Board of Directors consists of the Chairperson and the Directors.

### Article 25 (Classification and Convocation)

① The Board of Directors is divided into a Regular Board and an Extraordinary
Board, and is convened by the Chairperson.

② A Regular Board shall be convened no later than one month before the start of
each fiscal year, and an Extraordinary Board shall be convened when the
Chairperson deems it necessary.

③ The Chairperson shall notify each Director and Auditor in writing of the
convocation of the Board, specifying the agenda, date, and place, no later than
seven days before the meeting begins.

### Article 26 (Special Provisions on Convocation of the Board)

① The Chairperson shall convene the Board of Directors within 20 days from the
date of any of the following demands for convocation.

1. A demand by a majority of the Directors in office, stating the purpose of the
   meeting
2. A demand by an Auditor under Article 16, Paragraph ③, item 4

② If the person authorized to convene the Board is vacant or evades convening
it, making convocation impossible for seven days or more, the Board may be
convened with the approval of a majority of the Directors in office.

③ The chair of a Board convened under paragraph ② shall be elected from among
the attending Directors, with the eldest presiding.

### Article 27 (Prohibition of Written Resolutions)

Resolutions of the Board of Directors may not be adopted by written resolution.

### Article 28 (Quorum and Voting)

① The Board of Directors is opened upon the attendance of a majority of the
Directors in office, and resolutions are adopted by the approval of a majority
of the attending Directors. In case of a tie, the chair decides.

② Voting rights of the Board of Directors may not be delegated.

### Article 29 (Matters for Resolution by the Board of Directors)

The Board of Directors deliberates and resolves the following matters.

1. Matters concerning the execution of business
2. Matters concerning the operation of business plans
3. Matters concerning the preparation of the budget and settlement statements
4. Matters concerning amendment of the Articles of Incorporation
5. Matters concerning the management of property
6. Preparation of agenda items to be submitted to the General Assembly
7. Matters entrusted by the General Assembly
8. Matters within the authority of the Board under the Articles of Incorporation
9. Other matters that the Chairperson submits to the Board as important to the
   operation of the Society

## Chapter 6 Property and Accounting

### Article 30 (Classification of Property)

The property of the Society is classified into basic property and general
property as follows.

1. Basic property is the property contributed by the founders at the time of the
   Society's establishment and property designated as basic property by the
   Board of Directors. Its list is as shown in "Attachment 1."
2. General property is property other than basic property.

### Article 31 (Disposal of Basic Property, etc.)

To dispose of the basic property of the Society (including by sale, donation, or
exchange), the procedure for permission to amend the Articles of Incorporation
under Article 40 must be followed.

### Article 32 (Income)

The income of the Society shall be covered by member dues and other income.

### Article 33 (Fiscal Year)

The fiscal year of the Society follows the government fiscal year.

### Article 34 (Preparation of the Budget)

The revenue and expenditure budget of the Society shall be prepared no later
than one month before the start of each fiscal year, and shall be determined
upon a resolution of the Board of Directors and approval of the General
Assembly.

### Article 35 (Settlement of Accounts)

The Society shall prepare a settlement statement within two months after the end
of each fiscal year and obtain approval of the General Assembly upon a
resolution of the Board of Directors.

### Article 36 (Accounting Audit)

Auditors shall conduct accounting audits at least twice a year.

### Article 37 (Remuneration of Officers)

No remuneration shall be paid to officers, except Standing Directors who are
exclusively engaged in operating the business. However, actual expenses
necessary for performing duties may be paid.

## Chapter 7 Secretariat

### Article 38 (Secretariat)

① A Secretariat shall be established to handle the affairs of the Society under
the direction of the Chairperson.

② The Secretariat may have one Secretary General and the necessary staff.

③ The Secretary General shall be appointed by the Chairperson upon a resolution
of the Board of Directors.

④ Matters concerning the organization and operation of the Secretariat shall be
separately determined upon a resolution of the Board of Directors.

## Chapter 8 Miscellaneous Provisions

### Article 39 (Dissolution of the Society)

① If the Society intends to dissolve, it shall do so upon a resolution approved
by two-thirds or more of the members on the roll at the General Assembly, and
the dissolution shall be reported to the competent authority.

② Residual property upon the dissolution of the Society shall, upon a resolution
of the General Assembly, belong to the State, a local government, or another
non-profit corporation with a purpose similar to that of the Society.

### Article 40 (Amendment of the Articles of Incorporation)

To amend these Articles of Incorporation, a resolution approved by two-thirds or
more of the members on the roll at the General Assembly is required, and
permission from the competent authority must be obtained.

### Article 41 (Enactment of Rules)

Matters necessary for the operation of the Society other than those provided in
these Articles of Incorporation shall be determined by rules upon a resolution
of the Board of Directors.

## Addenda

### Article 1 (Effective Date)

These Articles of Incorporation take effect from the date they are registered
with the court after receiving permission from the competent authority.

### Article 2 (Transitional Provision)

Acts performed by the promoters, etc., for the establishment of the corporation
before these Articles of Incorporation took effect shall be deemed to have been
performed under these Articles of Incorporation.

### Article 3 (Signatures and Seals of Founders)

To establish the Society, these Articles of Incorporation have been prepared,
and all founders shall sign and affix their seals as follows.
